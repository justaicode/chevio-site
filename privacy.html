Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Chevio ("the app", "we", "us") is a nutrition and recipe tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We keep the app's data footprint small and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Information we collect

  • Account email. When you sign in, we collect your email address so we can send you a one-time login code and keep your data synced to your account. There is no password.
  • Profile details you enter. Sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and your goals — used to calculate your calorie and macro targets.
  • Your logged content. Foods, meals, recipes, daily food entries, and weight history that you create in the app.
  • Photos you add. If you attach a picture to a meal plan, that image is stored with your data. The app requests camera and photo-library access only for scanning barcodes and adding these pictures; it is not used otherwise.

We do not collect location, contacts, device identifiers for tracking, or usage analytics that identify you.

How your data is stored and synced

Your data is kept on your device and, once you sign in, synced to your private account so it is available across your devices. Each account's data is stored in its own record and is only accessible to that signed-in account.

Service providers we use

  • Supabase — hosts the account system and the synced database that stores your profile and logged content.
  • FairyMail — delivers the one-time sign-in code to your email address.
  • Cloudflare and our AI provider — when you use an AI feature (for example, generating or editing a recipe, or estimating a cooked weight), the relevant recipe text you submit is sent through a Cloudflare service to an AI model to produce the result. This text is processed to fulfil your request and is not used to build an advertising profile of you.
  • Open Food Facts — when you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts database to look up the product's nutrition information.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the app's features. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Data retention and deletion

  • You can reset all of the app's data from within the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account from within the app; this removes your account and the synced data associated with it.
  • You can sign out at any time, which stops syncing on that device.

Security

Data in transit is protected with standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your synced data is restricted to your authenticated account.

Children

Chevio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data directly in the app. If you would like help, or want a copy of the data associated with your account, contact us and we will assist.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
