Frequently asked

How do I sign in?

Enter your email and we'll send you a 6-digit code — there's no password. Type the code to sign in. Your foods, recipes, and progress then sync privately across your devices.

How are calories and macros calculated?

Chevio estimates your daily targets from your profile (sex, age, height, weight, activity, and goal). You can also set your own calorie and macro targets in Profile.

How do I log a recipe by weight?

Recipes show nutrition per 100 g, so you can weigh the finished dish and log the exact grams you eat. Chevio can also estimate the cooked weight for you.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your account and the data synced to it. You can also Reset all data to clear the app without deleting your account.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your synced data is tied to your account and isn't sold or used for advertising. See our Privacy Policy.
